Down payment rules

Please read these terms before you continue with a 20% down payment.

  1. 3-hour hold Your slot is held for 3 hours after the owner verifies your payment.
  2. Verification required The reservation stays temporary until your receipt is confirmed.
  3. Arrive within 3 hours If you do not arrive on time, the booking is cancelled and the slot is released.
  4. Temporary reservation A down payment does not confirm your stay until you check in on time.
