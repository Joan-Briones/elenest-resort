Resort Policies

Everything you need before you arrive.

Check-in & check-out

Present a valid government ID at reception. Late checkout may be arranged subject to availability and a modest fee.

Reservations & payment

A deposit may be required to confirm your dates. Accepted methods include cash and major cards as posted at the front desk.

Quoted rates apply to the room or package booked; incidental charges settle before departure unless otherwise arranged.

Pool & facilities

Swimming pool hours: 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM only. Other shared areas follow posted signage where noted.

Children under 12 must have an adult present at the pool deck.

Please shower before entering the pool, use proper swimwear, and follow any signage for events or maintenance.

Food & beverage

Outside food and beverages may be limited in certain venues or packages—ask reservations when booking events or catered stays.

We prioritize cleanliness and guest comfort; spills or leftovers in shared spaces should be cleared with staff assistance when needed.

Quiet hours & conduct

Quiet hours: typically 10:00 PM—7:00 AM. Keep music and gatherings at considerate levels.

Smoking—including e-cigarettes—is allowed only in designated outdoor areas. The resort reserves the right to refuse service for unsafe or disrespectful behaviour.

Pets & service animals

Pet policy varies by cottage or room category. Contact us before booking so we can confirm availability, fees, and house rules.

Trained service animals welcomed in line with applicable law—please advise us at booking for the best room placement.

Swimming pool policy details

Full swimming pool rules below. Hours or maintenance may change by season—front desk will confirm anything that affects your stay.

Hours, access & closing

Pool access for registered guests is 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM only. Please leave by closing so staff can secure the area. Short closures may occur for cleaning or repairs—follow signage and staff instructions.

Safety, supervision & behaviour

Children under 12 need an adult on the pool deck at all times. No running, unsafe diving, rough play, or glassware poolside. Intoxicated guests may be asked to leave the pool area.

Hygiene, swimwear & shared gear

Shower before entering and wear proper swimwear. Shared floats or life jackets should be used gently and returned to the rack; lost or damaged loaner items may be charged at replacement cost.

Weather, liability & personal items

The pool may close during storms or water-quality concerns. Swim at your own risk, keep valuables secured, and report slips or equipment problems to staff right away.
