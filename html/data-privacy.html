Data Privacy

How Elenest Resort collects, uses, and protects the information you share when you book, message us, or create an account. This notice follows the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Information we collect

When you book or create an account we ask for your name, phone number, and email. A reservation may also include stay dates, guest counts, the facility, your activity, arrival and departure times, and special requests.

We also keep messages you send, reviews and photos you choose to share, and the proof of payment you upload. At check-in, reception asks to see a valid government ID. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive the name and email those services share with us.

Why we use it

We use this information to confirm and manage your reservation, check payments, send booking updates, and reply to questions.

One-time codes are used only to confirm that a phone number or email belongs to you. Feedback you leave helps us look after the resort. We do not sell personal information.

Who can see it

Resort staff who handle your stay — reception, the manager, and admin — can see the details they need for that booking or message.

The website also uses service providers for accounts, sign-in, maps, and sending email or text codes. They receive only what is needed to perform that service.

How long we keep it

We keep booking, payment, and account records for as long as they are needed to complete your stay and maintain resort records.

You may ask us to correct details that are wrong, or to delete information we no longer need for your stay, payments, or legal records.

How we protect it

Access is limited to staff who look after reservations and guest care. Keep your password to yourself, and do not send it through the contact form.

Payment proofs and guest details are used for your booking only. Tell us right away if you think someone else has used your account.

Ask us about your data

To review, correct, or ask about your personal information, email elenest@gmail.com or call 09177906661. You can also send a message on the Contact Us page.

Please include the name and email or phone number on your booking so we can find the right record. This notice was last updated in November 2026.
